# IEEE Tribe Orientation 🌈

> **Find Your IEEE Tribe --- Discover how you like to contribute.**

## 📌 About the Project

**IEEE Tribe Orientation** is an interactive onboarding experience
designed to help students discover how they can contribute to an IEEE
team.

Instead of directly asking students which committee they want to join,
the experience presents creative situations and personality-style
choices. Students respond based on what excites them, how they like to
work, and the kind of contribution they enjoy.

The goal is to make IEEE orientation **creative, inclusive, memorable,
and engaging** rather than making it feel like a traditional
registration form.

## 💡 Concept: IEEE Identity Journey

The central idea is an **IEEE Identity Journey**.

Students scan a QR code with the title:

> **SCAN TO FIND YOUR IEEE ROLE**

The QR code opens a full-screen mobile-friendly experience containing
interactive cards, questions, and animations.

## 🚀 How It Works

### 1. Pick a Scene 🎭

Students are given realistic situations instead of committee names.

Example:

> **"A college event is happening tomorrow. What excites you the
> most?"**

Possible choices include:

-   🎨 Creating the poster everyone notices
-   📸 Capturing photos and reels
-   📝 Writing announcements and documentation
-   ⚙️ Making sure everything runs smoothly backstage

This allows students to choose based on their interests and behavior
rather than selecting a committee directly.

### 2. Choose Your Superpower ✨

Students answer:

> **"Which compliment would you love to hear?"**

Examples:

-   ✨ "Your design looks amazing."
-   🎤 "You communicate ideas really well."
-   📷 "Your content made the event feel alive."
-   🧠 "You solved the problem before anyone noticed it."
-   🚀 "You took initiative and led the team."

## 🎯 Objectives

-   Make IEEE orientation more engaging for new students.
-   Help students discover their strengths and interests.
-   Introduce IEEE opportunities without immediately presenting
    committee names.
-   Create a memorable first interaction with the IEEE community.
-   Turn orientation into an interactive experience rather than a
    registration form.

## ✨ Key Features

-   📱 QR-code based entry
-   🎭 Scenario-based questions
-   🃏 Interactive cards
-   ✨ Creative personality/role identities
-   🎨 Visual and engaging presentation
-   🌈 Inclusive onboarding experience
-   🧭 Personalized direction toward suitable IEEE teams

## 👥 Intended Users

The primary users are **students participating in IEEE orientation** who
want to understand where their interests and skills could fit within the
IEEE community.
